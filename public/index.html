What do these switches do?

They save your preferences in TEDDY-XMD. They do not view Stories or send reactions. TikTok’s official developer APIs currently cover profile and video display, but do not document Story viewing or Story reactions. A Heroku server cannot operate the TikTok app on your phone in the background.
